# Carmen — Marketing Channels Research

*Last updated: April 2026*

This is a curated list of communities, creators, and publications where songwriters gather — in English (primarily US/Canada/UK) and French (Quebec and France). It's meant to seed your Notion tracker, not replace it.

**Key principle**: Most songwriter communities are hostile to self-promo. The winning play is almost never "hey I made this thing, check it out." It's to be a genuine participant — share craft insights, comment thoughtfully, mention Carmen in your profile/bio. The AI-app angle especially triggers skepticism, so lean on "tool built by songwriters for songwriters" more than "AI-powered."

## TIER 3 — LONG TAIL

Don't focus here until Tiers 1 and 2 are exhausted.

- Smaller Discord servers (search "songwriting Discord" — there are 50+)
- TikTok songwriter creators (partnership deals, mostly paid)
- YouTube cover artists with engaged songwriting-curious audiences
- Substack songwriting newsletters (Lyrics I Love, How Great Songs Are Made, etc.)
- Medium publications tagged "Songwriting" (editorial pitches)
- Songwriting classes at community colleges / music schools (direct-to-educator outreach)
- Songwriter retreats (Durango Songwriters Expo, etc.) — expensive but high-intent
- Church worship-music communities (high-volume lyricists, underserved)
- Poetry communities (AllPoetry, r/Poetry) — crossover audience

## WHAT TO POST

Same framing wins everywhere:

1. **Craft insight posts**: "I've been studying how [famous songwriter] handles prosody in their bridges, and..."
2. **Tool reveals in context**: Share a specific before/after of a rewrite and *casually* mention Carmen. "Here's a messy verse I wrote this morning, and here's the same verse after I used Carmen's 'fix the flow' tool. Notice how the stressed syllables now line up with the downbeats."
3. **Founder story / building in public**: On Indie Hackers, Twitter, and Hacker News — how and why Carmen was built. Transparency about decisions (why Haiku 4.5, why single-file HTML, why no subscription tier below Creator).
4. **Direct help**: Answer other people's craft questions with real insight. Mention Carmen only if it's actually relevant. Build karma and reputation.

## WHAT TO AVOID

- "AI-powered" as the headline framing (writers are exhausted by this). Lead with craft.
- Posting the same content to multiple channels simultaneously — communities notice and penalize it.
- Any variant of "Hey everyone, check out my new app!" in a songwriter subreddit. Instant downvote.
- Astroturfing. Don't have friends make fake enthusiastic posts. Songwriters have unusually good bullshit detectors.

## SEASONAL TIMING

- **January** — songwriters set annual goals; high intent for tools
- **March–May** — pre-summer creative burst
- **September** — "back to school" / "back to the craft" moment
- **October/November** — pre-holiday push if you have a gift-able offer

Avoid: mid-June to mid-August (summer slowdown), mid-December (holiday noise).
